# Community expectations

Be respectful, specific and constructive in issues, reviews and discussions.
Critique the work rather than the person. Harassment, threats, discrimination,
sexualized conduct, doxxing and disclosure of another person's private information
are unacceptable. Respect maintainers' decisions about scope and moderation.

These expectations apply in project-managed spaces. Maintainers may edit or
remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation. Moderation
should be proportionate to the behavior and protect the people involved.

The maintainer is [DavidHLP](https://github.com/DavidHLP). Use contact details the
maintainer publishes on that profile for private conduct concerns. If no private
contact is listed, request a private contact channel in a minimal repository issue
without naming people or publishing evidence. This document does not invent a
private email address or promise a response deadline. Security vulnerabilities
have a separate verified reporting channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
